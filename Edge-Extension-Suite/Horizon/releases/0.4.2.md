## What's in it

- **Backgrounds:** Bing daily image (4K where supported), Windows Spotlight and Unsplash photos; favorites,
  your own uploads, gradients; daily or per-tab rotation
- **Layouts:** seven arrangements of the page (Classic, Editorial, Glass card, Horizon band, Tiles, Ring,
  Dashboard), chosen from the **Layout** button at the bottom left with a live preview as you hover, from
  *Settings → Appearance*, or with `Y`. Weather and upcoming-dates cards, live tiles, shortcut docks and an
  optional tagline of your own. Layouts start once Horizon knows your name (the first new tab asks for it)
- **Clock and dashboard:** clock with the day and date beneath it (four formats in Classic; the other layouts
  show the date their own way), greeting or mantras, main focus, daily
  quote, world clocks, search, quick links
- **Daily Bible verse** as the quote (or mixed with quotes): KJV built in; WEB, ASV, BBE; ESV with your own key;
  NKJV, NIV, NLT, NASB via Bible Gateway
- **Photo button:** New photo, Favorite, and Keep this (pin a background so it doesn't change)
- **Floating windows:** borderless YouTube, Spotify or almost any site floating over the new tab, several at once; drag,
  resize, maximize or pop out above other apps (`Shift+W`)
- **Board:** a pinned tab of mini browsers in a grid (1 to 6 tiles, several boards) that keeps playing in the
  background; embed-friendly sites only or *Load almost any site* (`G`)
- **Axentra Spaces link:** optionally send windows to the [Axentra Spaces](../../../Spaces/) desktop app; Outlook,
  Gmail, Google Calendar and Teams opened as a floating window or Board tile go there instead, because they can't
  run inside a page
- **Weather:** 6-day forecast; city search with suggestions; device location with an automatic approximate
  fallback when Windows Location services are off
- **Reminders:** birthdays, anniversaries, bills, events; yearly/monthly/weekly repeats; notifications up to a
  month ahead; dashboard countdowns; `.ics` import and export
- **Habits:** schedules, streaks, completion rate, heatmap, daily reminders
- **Tab stash:** from the new tab, the toolbar button, `Alt+Shift+S` or right-click
- **Productivity:** todo lists, notes, focus timer with notifications, breathing exercises, zen mode,
  soundscapes
- **Manage favorites from the bar:** right-click to open in a new tab, edit, or delete (with undo, folders included);
  *Add this page to favorites* and *Manage all favorites…* in the website bar's ⋯ menu
- **Favorites bar** with folders on the new tab and, by default, on every website (always visible, moving the
  page down, or sliding down; see-through glass or solid; per-site choices,
  `Alt+Shift+F` to show or hide it)
- New Axentra Horizon logo, matching the Axentra suite
- Links to local files and network servers with an up/down indicator
- **Backup & restore:** automatic daily backups to your OneDrive or Google Drive folder (last 30 kept, uploaded
  photos included) and one-click restore from a dated list; manual export and import
- Keyboard shortcuts, settings synced across devices, and the running version shown faintly in the bottom-left
  corner
- Floating windows and the Board keep working after Edge turns the extension off and on again

## Known limitations

- The website favorites bar can't appear on `edge://` pages, the Add-ons store or PDFs. On a site whose layout
  doesn't suit an always-visible bar, choose *Slide down* or *Hide on this site* from the bar's ⋯ menu.
- Not yet on the Microsoft Edge Add-ons store; install unpacked (see the [README](../README.md#installation)).
  Edge may offer to turn off developer-mode extensions at startup; see
  [If Edge turns Horizon off](../README.md#if-edge-turns-horizon-off). The store release is planned ([store/](../store/)).
- Precise weather location needs Windows Location services turned on; otherwise the location is approximate
  (city level) unless you choose a city.
- Soundscapes play while a new tab page is open in the browser.
- Floating windows close with their new tab; use the Board or Axentra Spaces for windows that stay open.
- Outlook, Gmail, Google Calendar and Teams can't load inside a page once Edge has saved them for offline use, so
  they open in Axentra Spaces. Banks and some sign-in pages refuse frames too.
- On YouTube in a floating window, Edge 155 occasionally crashes the video frame (a grey sad face). The crash is
  inside Edge itself; click ↻ in the window's bar to reload it, or watch in Axentra Spaces.
- Permission note: *declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess* is used only for frames inside Horizon's own pages (the
  Board and floating windows); see [Privacy](../PRIVACY.md). Those frames get only media-player permissions (no
  camera, microphone, location, screen capture or clipboard reading).
