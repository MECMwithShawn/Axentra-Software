## What's new

- **Ribbon laid out like the major commercial PDF editors:** Home, Convert, Review, Page Layout, Forms,
  Share, Erase, Protect, Accessibility, View and Help, with Hand and Edit Text on every tab.
- **Compress PDF:** Extreme, Recommended and Less compression, each showing the real resulting size before
  you choose; apply it in place (undoable) or save a compressed copy. **Compress Files** compresses many PDFs
  at once into `_compressed` copies, never overwriting the originals.
- **MSI installer** for silent and managed deployment, alongside the .exe installer.

## Fixes

- Password-protected PDFs can be digitally signed; the file stays encrypted and is not rewritten. Signatures
  in encrypted documents are now validated.
- Radio-button groups: Required and Read-only apply to the whole group, and any selected option satisfies a
  required group. Editing a radio button's properties no longer selects it.
- Tab Order and the accessibility check now cover pages that contain only form fields.
- Editing text keeps centred and right-aligned text aligned, and keeps the fonts and colours of lines that mix
  styles.
- Editing OCR'd text on a scanned page replaces the scanned words; before, the original words stayed visible
  underneath the new text.

## Known limitations

- The installers are not code-signed yet (see [security](../security/virustotal.md#code-signing)).
- Saving a digitally signed document invalidates its signatures (you are warned first; sign last).
- PDF/A output needs Ghostscript and is best-effort; validate with veraPDF when compliance matters.
