Released 2026-10-22 as a GitHub **pre-release** (tag `outlook-v0.1.4`). Linux only. Upgrades 0.1.0–0.1.3 in
place; your data and signed-in accounts are kept.

## What's new

Your Microsoft account photo on your account avatars; one window per profile (launching again from Axentra
Command or the app menu shows the open window instead of a second copy); no more lost letters or misplaced keys
while typing on a busy machine; addresses never dropped from To/Cc/Bcc or event attendees; and dozens of
polish fixes from four review rounds on the desktop engine. Quit Axentra Outlook before installing. Plus everything in 0.1.3 (resizable dialogs and movable pop-out windows), 0.1.2
(merged Outlook-style title bar) and 0.1.1 (encrypted monthly backups to OneDrive, remembered Microsoft client
ID). See the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md).

## Build

- Source: [`../source/`](../source/), snapshot of commit `fdf55ab`.
- `cargo build --release --locked -p axentra-desktop` (rustc 1.99.1, Zorin OS 18.1 x86_64), then
  `python3 packaging/linux/package.py --output dist`. No build warnings in debug or release.
- Package `axentra-outlook` 0.1.4-1, amd64, about 10 MB (40 MB installed). Same dependencies as 0.1.0.
