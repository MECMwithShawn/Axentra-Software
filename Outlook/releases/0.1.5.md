place; your data and signed-in accounts are kept. Quit Axentra Outlook before installing.

## What's new

Glass (see-through window backgrounds), Pin to desktop (a full-screen mail view that stays behind your other
windows), readable HTML email in dark mode, and local, advisory junk email handling: per-account block and
never-block rules, undoable reports, warning signs for phishing, and learning from your reports. See the
[changelog](../CHANGELOG.md).

## Build

- Source: [`../source/`](../source/), snapshot of commit `5923b18`.
- `cargo build --release --locked -p axentra-desktop` (rustc 1.99.1, Zorin OS 18.1 x86_64), then
  `python3 packaging/linux/package.py --output dist`. No build or clippy warnings.
- Package `axentra-outlook` 0.1.5-1, amd64, about 10 MB (41 MB installed).

## Status

- 645 automated tests passed. The junk filter was audited externally and every finding fixed; its learning check
  is narrower than the original plan (documented in `source/docs/JUNK.md`). Glass, Pin to desktop and dark-mode
  email were checked by the user with debug builds on GNOME (Wayland). Not code-signed; VirusTotal scan pending
  (see [security](../security/virustotal.md)).
- Known limits: [LINUX.md](../LINUX.md#remaining-work-and-requirements).
